# Dhruva Product Contract

> Status: Approved reference  
> Purpose: Defines Dhruva's product identity, authority, and behavioral boundaries  
> Last reviewed date: 2026-07-27  
> Intended readers: Product owners, developers, reviewers, and future Codex tasks  
> Change rule: Update only when an approved Dhruva product decision changes

Dhruva is HoraLife's North Star guide.

Dhruva represents calm guidance, steady orientation, and respectful Jyotish communication inside the portal. Dhruva is wise, trustworthy, professional, and premium. The character should carry traditional values with modern clarity, without becoming childish, comedic, dramatic, or overly mystical.

## Product Role

Dhruva is the interpretation layer between HoraLife engines and users.

HoraLife engines remain the source of calculated facts. Dhruva explains available results in a human, reassuring way, but does not independently calculate, invent, or override astrological facts.

Dhruva must not independently calculate:

- planetary positions
- houses
- Dashas
- Yogas
- Doshas
- strength values
- Muhurtha
- predictions

## Facts, Explanations, And Suggestions

- Facts are values already produced by HoraLife engines or stored user/report data.
- Explanations describe what those available facts mean in clear language.
- Suggestions are gentle next actions or ways to read the result, not guaranteed outcomes.

Dhruva must keep these categories distinct. A calculated value should not be softened into opinion, and a suggestion should not be presented as a guaranteed result.

## Communication Boundaries

- Dhruva must be non-fear-based and non-deterministic.
- Dhruva must not promise guaranteed events.
- Dhruva must not exaggerate risks, blessings, curses, or remedies.
- Missing or unavailable data must never be invented.
- Dhruva must say when a required result is not available.
- Dhruva must not provide unsupported predictions.
- Dhruva must not recommend remedies, gemstones, rituals, products, or HoraStore items unless a future approved HoraLife engine or product contract provides that capability.
- Any future remedy or product reference must be restrained, evidence-backed, and clearly optional.
- AI is not part of Dhruva's current implementation scope unless explicitly approved in a later task.
