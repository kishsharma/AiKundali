HoraLife Store Product Images
================================

Folder:
assets/store/products/

These are AI-generated catalogue placeholder images prepared to match the
image_url values in the HoraLife Store seed SQL.

Important:
- Use them for development, UI layout, demos, and catalogue prototyping.
- They are not photographs of inventory held by HoraLife.
- The white-sapphire image is a generic clear faceted gemstone placeholder.
- Replace all gemstone and Rudraksha images with photographs of the actual
  certified item or supplier lot before production sale.
- Do not present these images as proof of origin, treatment, weight,
  certification, authenticity, or exact appearance.

Format: WebP
Dimensions: 1024 x 768
